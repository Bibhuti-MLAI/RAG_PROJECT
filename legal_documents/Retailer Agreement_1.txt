Retailer Agreement 1

This Agreement is made and entered into as of January 1, 2025, by and between MedPharma Inc., a company organized and existing under the laws of California with its principal place of business at 123 Pharma Lane, Los Angeles, CA ("Company"), and HealthPlus Distributors LLC, a company organized and existing under the laws of Texas with its principal place of business at 789 Wellness Ave, Dallas, TX ("Distributor").

1. Appointment:
1.1 Company appoints Distributor as a retailer of its pharmaceutical products in the territory of Texas.
1.2 Distributor agrees to purchase and sell the Products in Texas, subject to this Agreement.

2. Products:
2.1 Products include prescription and over-the-counter medicines listed in Exhibit A.
2.2 Company may add or remove products with reasonable notice to Distributor.

3. Territory:
3.1 Distributor shall only sell Products in Texas.
3.2 Distributor shall not sell Products outside Texas without prior written consent from Company.

4. Purchase and Sale:
4.1 Distributor purchases Products at prices set forth in Exhibit B.
4.2 Company ships Products per Exhibit B.
4.3 Distributor handles storage, handling, and distribution.

5. Pricing and Payment:
5.1 Pricing is in Exhibit B.
5.2 Payment terms follow Exhibit B.

6. Term and Termination:
6.1 Agreement term is 2 years unless terminated earlier.
6.2 Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice.
6.3 Company may terminate immediately if Distributor breaches material provisions.

7. Confidentiality:
7.1 Distributor must keep Company information confidential.

8. Compliance with Laws:
8.1 Distributor complies with pharmaceutical laws.

9. Warranty and Liability:
9.1 Company warrants Products free from material defects for 90 days from shipment.
9.2 Liability is limited to the purchase price of Products.

10. Entire Agreement:
10.1 This Agreement supersedes prior communications.

11. Governing Law:
11.1 Governed by California law.
