Retailer Agreement 4

This Agreement is made and entered into as of June 1, 2025, by and between Aurelix Biotech Inc., a company organized and existing under the laws of New Jersey with its principal place of business at 250 Meridian Parkway, Princeton, NJ ("Company"), and NorthStar Pharmacy Distributors LLC, a company organized and existing under the laws of Florida with its principal place of business at 77 Bayview Drive, Miami, FL ("Distributor").

1. Appointment:
1.1 Company appoints Distributor as the exclusive retailer of its pharmaceutical products in the territory of Florida.
1.2 Distributor agrees to purchase and sell the Products in Florida, subject to this Agreement.

2. Products:
2.1 Products include prescription antibiotics, respiratory care medicines and vaccines listed in Exhibit A.
2.2 Company may add or remove products with 30 days' written notice to Distributor.

3. Territory:
3.1 Distributor shall only sell Products in Florida.
3.2 Distributor shall not sell Products outside Florida without prior written consent from Company.
3.3 Company shall not appoint any other retailer for the Products in Florida during the term.

4. Purchase and Sale:
4.1 Distributor purchases Products at prices set forth in Exhibit B.
4.2 Company ships Products FOB Princeton, NJ within 7 business days of receiving a purchase order.
4.3 Distributor handles storage, handling, and distribution; vaccines must be kept in a monitored cold chain at 2°C to 8°C.
4.4 Distributor shall purchase Products worth at least USD 500,000 in each contract year.

5. Pricing and Payment:
5.1 Pricing is in Exhibit B.
5.2 Payment shall be made within 60 days of invoice receipt.
5.3 Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.
5.4 Company may revise prices once per year with 60 days' written notice.

6. Term and Termination:
6.1 Agreement term is 5 years unless terminated earlier, and renews automatically for 1-year periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.
6.2 Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice.
6.3 Company may terminate immediately if Distributor breaches material provisions or fails to meet the minimum purchase requirement in Section 4.4 for two consecutive years.

7. Confidentiality:
7.1 Distributor must keep Company information confidential during the term and for 3 years after termination.

8. Compliance with Laws:
8.1 Distributor complies with pharmaceutical laws, including FDA regulations and the Drug Supply Chain Security Act (DSCSA).
8.2 Distributor shall report any adverse drug event to Company within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.

9. Recalls:
9.1 Company shall notify Distributor of any product recall within 24 hours.
9.2 Company bears the cost of recalls caused by manufacturing defects.

10. Warranty and Liability:
10.1 Company warrants Products free from material defects for 180 days from shipment.
10.2 Liability is limited to the purchase price of Products, except for liability arising from Company's negligence in manufacturing.

11. Entire Agreement:
11.1 This Agreement supersedes prior communications.

12. Governing Law:
12.1 Governed by New Jersey law.

Signed,
Aurelix Biotech Inc.
By: Robert Hayes, CEO

Exhibit A: Products
- Amoxicillin 500 mg capsules (bottle of 30)
- Azithromycin 250 mg tablets (pack of 6)
- Salbutamol inhaler 100 mcg
- Influenza vaccine, single-dose vial (cold-chain)

Exhibit B: Pricing and Terms
- Amoxicillin 500 mg: USD 9.50 per bottle
- Azithromycin 250 mg: USD 12.00 per pack
- Salbutamol inhaler: USD 18.75 per inhaler
- Influenza vaccine: USD 14.20 per vial
- Payment: 60 days from invoice; minimum annual purchase USD 500,000
