Retailer Agreement 3

This Agreement is made and entered into as of March 15, 2025, by and between Zenovia Pharmaceuticals Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 with its registered office at 42 Andheri Industrial Estate, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400053, India ("Company"), and CarePoint Retail Pharmacies Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 with its registered office at 18 MG Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560001, India ("Retailer").

1. Appointment:
1.1 Company appoints Retailer as a non-exclusive retailer of its pharmaceutical products in the territory of Karnataka.
1.2 Retailer agrees to purchase and sell the Products through its licensed retail pharmacy outlets in Karnataka, subject to this Agreement.

2. Products:
2.1 Products include prescription medicines, over-the-counter medicines and diabetes care products listed in Exhibit A.
2.2 Company may add or remove products with 45 days' written notice to Retailer.

3. Territory:
3.1 Retailer shall only sell Products in Karnataka.
3.2 Retailer shall not sell Products outside Karnataka, including through online channels, without prior written consent from Company.

4. Purchase and Sale:
4.1 Retailer purchases Products at prices set forth in Exhibit B.
4.2 Company ships Products within 5 business days of receiving a purchase order.
4.3 Retailer shall store Products at the temperatures stated on the label; cold-chain Products must be stored between 2°C and 8°C.
4.4 Minimum order value is INR 50,000 per purchase order.

5. Pricing and Payment:
5.1 Pricing is in Exhibit B.
5.2 Payment shall be made within 45 days of invoice receipt.
5.3 Late payments attract interest at 1.5% per month on the outstanding amount.
5.4 Retailer receives a 2% discount for payment made within 10 days of invoice receipt.

6. Term and Termination:
6.1 Agreement term is 3 years unless terminated earlier.
6.2 Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.
6.3 Company may terminate immediately if Retailer's drug license is suspended or cancelled, or if Retailer breaches material provisions.
6.4 On termination, Company shall buy back unexpired, undamaged Products at 90% of the invoice price.

7. Confidentiality:
7.1 Retailer must keep Company pricing and business information confidential during the term and for 2 years after termination.

8. Compliance with Laws:
8.1 Retailer shall comply with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and all applicable pharmacy regulations.
8.2 Prescription medicines shall be dispensed only against a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner.

9. Returns and Expiry:
9.1 Retailer may return Products expiring within 90 days for full credit.
9.2 Damaged Products must be reported within 7 days of delivery.

10. Warranty and Liability:
10.1 Company warrants Products free from material defects until the expiry date printed on the pack.
10.2 Liability is limited to the purchase price of the affected Products.

11. Entire Agreement:
11.1 This Agreement supersedes prior communications.

12. Governing Law and Disputes:
12.1 Governed by the laws of India.
12.2 Disputes shall be settled by arbitration in Mumbai under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Signed,
Zenovia Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
By: Arjun Mehta, Managing Director
CarePoint Retail Pharmacies Pvt. Ltd.
By: Kavya Rao, Chief Operating Officer

- Metformin 500 mg tablets (strip of 10)
- Atorvastatin 10 mg tablets (strip of 10)
- Paracetamol 650 mg tablets (strip of 15)
- Insulin Glargine 100 IU/ml pen (cold-chain)
- Blood glucose test strips (pack of 50)

- Metformin 500 mg: INR 18 per strip
- Atorvastatin 10 mg: INR 42 per strip
- Paracetamol 650 mg: INR 25 per strip
- Insulin Glargine pen: INR 610 per pen
- Glucose test strips: INR 720 per pack
- Payment: 45 days from invoice; 2% early-payment discount within 10 days
